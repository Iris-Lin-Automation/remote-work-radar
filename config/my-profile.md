### E-commerce & Operations

- Product listing, order management, after-sales
- KOL/influencer coordination basics

### Data & Tools

- Notion, Feishu/Lark, Confluence

### Languages
- Mandarin Chinese (native)
- English (professional working proficiency)
- Can handle bilingual (EN/ZH) communication

## Hard Requirements
- **Full-time remote only** (no on-site / no relocation)
- Open to: full-time, contract, part-time (≥20h/week)
- Timezone: UTC+8 (Beijing/Taipei) — can overlap 4h with US/EU teams

## Strong Positive Signals (score higher)
- Company based in Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, or remote-first
- Mandarin/Chinese speaker preferred or required
- Mentions salary range
- Direct contact info (email / WeChat / Google Form)
- Keywords: 台湾, 跨境, 远程, remote, QA, e-commerce, TikTok, cross-border, Mandarin

## Negative Signals (score lower or reject)
- Requires on-site / office presence in specific city
- Requires 5+ years experience for a senior role
- US/EU timezone only with no Asia overlap
- Requires specific local work permit (US green card, EU citizenship etc.)
